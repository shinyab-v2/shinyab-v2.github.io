Food Taste & Texture Idioms

음식의 맛·식감·풍미를 자연스럽게 설명하는 어휘와 이디엄

1. 기본 맛 · Taste

단어의미예문
sour / tangy신 / 상큼하게 톡 쏘는This lemonade is too sour.
bitter쓴Dark chocolate tastes bitter.
spicy / mild매운 / 순한Could you make it less spicy?
sweet단It's sweet but not too sweet.
salty짠These fries are too salty.
savory / umami짭짤한 풍미 / 감칠맛The broth has a rich, savory flavor.

2. 식감 · Texture

표현뜻함께 쓰는 음식
crispy얇고 바삭한chicken skin, coating
crunchy와작와작 씹히는chips, nuts
juicy육즙이 풍부한fried chicken, steak
tender연하고 부드러운beef, chicken
chewy쫄깃한rice cakes, noodles
creamy크림처럼 부드러운soup, pasta
flaky얇은 층이 바스러지는croissant
soggy눅눅한fries
greasy기름기가 많은fried food
tough / dry질긴 / 퍽퍽한overcooked meat

3. 이디엄과 자연스러운 표현

mouth-watering

군침이 도는

That fried chicken looks mouth-watering.

finger-licking good

손가락까지 핥을 만큼 맛있는

These wings are finger-licking good.

hit the spot

딱 원하던 맛이다

That cold drink really hit the spot.

melt in your mouth

입에서 살살 녹다

The beef melts in your mouth.

to die for

정말 끝내주는

Their cheesecake is to die for.

packed with flavor

풍미가 가득한

This curry is packed with flavor.

bursting with flavor

풍미가 폭발하는 듯한

These dumplings are bursting with flavor.

an acquired taste

익숙해져야 좋아지는 맛

Durian is an acquired taste for many people.

not my cup of tea

내 취향이 아니다

Extremely spicy food isn't my cup of tea.

leave a bad taste in your mouth

불쾌한 인상을 남기다

The rude service left a bad taste in my mouth.

4. 실전 식당 영어

The coating is perfectly crispy, and the meat is juicy.
튀김옷은 바삭하고 고기는 촉촉해요.

It's a bit too greasy for me.
제 입맛에는 조금 느끼해요.

It's a little bland. It could use more seasoning.
조금 싱거워요. 간이 더 필요해요.

That meal really hit the spot.
그 식사는 정말 딱 좋았어요.

Q3. 두리안은 익숙해져야 좋아지는 맛이라고 할 때?

Durian is an acquired taste.
